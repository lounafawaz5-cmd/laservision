# ZVC Content Calendar: board guidelines (snapshot)

Board: https://trello.com/b/PFGrFOmn/zvc-content-calendar
Snapshot taken 2026-10-22 from the Guidelines column. The board is the source of truth.

## START HERE: how this board works

**One card = one post.** Cards start in the calendar column and move right as they progress.

**Status is the column.** Drag the card: Planned > Shot > Editing > In Review > Ready to Post > Posted. Nothing else to update. Georges approves in In Review.

**Calendar view.** Open the Calendar power-up at the top of the board to see every post by date, whatever column it is in. This is the planning view.

**Labels.** Pillar (what job the post does), format (Reel or Carousel), platforms (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok). PINNED stays at the top of the profile. PLACEHOLDER means the card is waiting on an answer and has a fallback in its alternates.

**Checklists on every card.** Alternate topics: swap the topic, keep the slot doing the same job. Ready to post: caption, cover picture, subtitles, Georges's approval.

**Filming guide.** Every card links to its format card in the Guidelines column. Click it before filming.

**Ideas & brain dump.** Luna's space. Nothing is committed until it gets a date and moves into the calendar.

**Cadence.** ZEISS Vision Center: 3 posts a week (Tue, Thu, Sat) in October and November, 4 a week from December (Monday added: seasonal and gifting). Stories daily Mon to Sat.

## Brand rules: what each account never does

**LaserVision informs, reassures and directs. It never sells.**
No retail language, no offers, no prices. Pillars: Educational 50%, Credibility 30%, Storytelling 20%. CTA on every post: WhatsApp to book (until laservision.me is rebuilt).

**ZEISS Vision Center styles and solves everyday vision problems. It never treats.**
No medical claims, no diagnosis language. No ZEISS logo in original content. Lens types described by what they do, not by ZEISS product names. Pillars: Style and identity 40%, Product and problem-solving 35%, Seasonal and in-store 25%. CTA on every post: WhatsApp 81 47 20 20.

**The handoff.** Under-12s and anything medical go from ZVC to LaserVision next door. Frames and lenses go from LaserVision to ZVC. Show the walk between the two whenever it is natural.

**Platforms.** Everything goes on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. LaserVision carousels and educational posts also go on LinkedIn. ZVC is not on LinkedIn.

**Language.** Film in Arabic, subtitle in English until Marc and Georges confirm otherwise.

## Stories: the daily rhythm (both accounts)

Same job every weekday so nobody has to think about it. Phone clip or a single frame, under ten minutes to make. Sunday off on both accounts.

**Mon**
LV: Today in the clinic. A corridor, a room being set up, a machine switched on. No voice, text on screen.
ZVC: New on the shelf. Whatever arrived. Fast pan, brand visible, no ZEISS logo.

**Tue**
LV: Tuesday's feed post reposted with a watch sticker.
ZVC: Try-on of the day. Luna or a staff member, one frame, yes-or-no poll.

**Wed**
LV: One question, one answer. A reception question answered in one frame.
ZVC: The four questions. One of: how much, delivery time, when can I collect, what brands. Ends "WhatsApp us your prescription". The only story slot with a CTA.

**Thu**
LV: Meet a face. A recurring staff member, name and role, one line. Fallback: a room.
ZVC: Thursday's feed post reposted.

**Fri**
LV: Friday's feed post reposted.
ZVC: Which one. Two frames, one face, poll. The most repeatable engagement format the store has.

**Sat**
LV: Ask us. Question box. Answers go out the following Wednesday.
ZVC: Customer moment. Someone leaving with new frames, with consent. Fallback: a staff pick.

**Rule.** Anything that needs consent has a same-day fallback. Stories keep the account alive and feed WhatsApp. They never replace the feed post's job.

## Try-on formats: Three frames one face, Face shape, Before and after

_This is one way to do it, not the only way. If a better idea comes up on the day, take it. What matters is the job the post does._

**What it is.** The core ZVC format. One face, a few frames, one pick. Everything else is a variation.

**The GIF feel.** Three or four photos or micro-clips of different people with different face shapes trying frames, cut fast to music, almost a GIF. Not a talking head. The rhythm is the content.

**Three frames, one face.** Frame 1, 2, 3 on the beat, then the pick with the reason in ten words as text.

**Face shape, frame shape.** Opens with the hook ("I have a round face and I've been lied to for years"), then the same fast try-on, ends with the rule of thumb for that face shape.

**Before and after.** The frames someone walked in with, the frames they left with. Two shots, one cut, a reaction. Consent needed. Fallback: staff or Luna's own.

**Shot.** At the mirror or against a plain wall in store. Luna's hand can swap the frames in frame. 15 to 25 seconds.

**Graphics.** Frame and brand as a small tag on each try-on. The pick gets the big card. Price never shown.

**Ends with.** "Come try them. WhatsApp 81 47 20 20."

## Product and problem-solving explainers

_This is one way to do it, not the only way. If a better idea comes up on the day, take it. What matters is the job the post does._

**What it is.** One everyday problem, one honest answer, one lens that fixes it. Screens all day, night driving, progressives, contacts, kids.

**Do it as a sketch.** A scene, acted out, 20 to 30 seconds. We are visually in the problem, then visually in the fix. Someone squinting at a laptop at 6pm, rubbing their eyes, then the same shot with the right lenses and a normal face. Someone in a car at night flinching at headlights, then not. A parent watching a kid squint at the TV. Simple, straightforward, a little funny.

**Structure.** Problem shown (5 to 10 seconds). One line naming it. Fix shown (10 seconds). One line on what the lens does. CTA.

**Words.** Solution named by what it does ("lenses made for screens", "lenses that cut glare at night"), never by the ZEISS product name. No ZEISS logo.

**Tone.** Honest to the point of being disarming. "Blue light glasses won't fix your sleep. Here's what they actually do."

**Kids and anything medical.** Send to LaserVision next door for the test. ZVC never diagnoses.

**Ends with.** "Bring your prescription or WhatsApp it to us. 81 47 20 20."

## Rate my glasses (street)

_This is one way to do it, not the only way. If a better idea comes up on the day, take it. What matters is the job the post does._

**What it is.** Street interviews. Luna stops people in Hazmieh and scores their frames out of ten, with a reason. Fast, funny, kind. Ends with "come get scored properly, link in bio".

**Shot.** Handheld, vertical, Luna in frame with the person. Three to five people per video, five seconds each. Big score number pops on screen with a sound.

**Rules.** Never below a 6. The joke is about the frame, never the face. Consent on camera from each person before it is used.

**Fallback if street filming is not cleared.** Staff edition: the team's own frames scored. Or the optometrists' own frames.

**Second edition (week 7).** Same format exactly so it becomes recognisable.

## Staff styling battle

_This is one way to do it, not the only way. If a better idea comes up on the day, take it. What matters is the job the post does._

**What it is.** The two optometrists each pick a frame for the same person. Played as a sketch: they compete, they argue a little, the customer is caught in the middle. Comments decide the winner.

**Shot.** Same person, same spot, two takes, one wearing each pick. Each optometrist gets three seconds to defend their choice to camera, ideally over the top. 20 to 30 seconds.

**Graphics.** Side A / Side B with the optometrist's name. "Vote in the comments" as the final card.

**Variations.** Luna vs optometrist. Customer picks vs staff picks.

**Follow-up.** Announce the winner in a story two days later. First post that asks for engagement.

## Seasonal and in-store (new arrivals, ZEISS base layer, trend of the month)

_This is one way to do it, not the only way. If a better idea comes up on the day, take it. What matters is the job the post does._

**This is where ZVC gets to play.** Sketches, trends, and the kind of idea that is so clever it cannot be cringe. Each seasonal post is its own creative brief; these are the starting points.

**Back to school.** Someone wakes up in a panic, thinks they're late for school, scrambles, and it turns out to be a 50-year-old nurse. Cut to: kids' frames and the eye test next door. Sketch, 20 seconds.

**Glacier Optics / winter.** Luna on top of a mountain in the snow. Zoom in on her, zoom out, she is alone in all that white, she skis down, and it lands on the frames. Built so the reveal is the joke. Cute, not cringe, because it is done well.

**New arrival.** Unboxing, first try-on, brand visible. 20 seconds. Needs the arrival date confirmed.

**Trend of the month.** Ride whatever trend is running on Instagram that month with frames as the subject. ZVC should do a lot of trends; they are cheap and they travel.

**ZEISS base layer.** Whatever ZEISS has scheduled that month, reshot with our store and our people. No ZEISS logo in the original cut. Fallback: in-store moment of the week.

**Eye test next door.** The loop shown physically: test at LaserVision, walk over, pick frames at ZVC. One person, one visit, one continuous story. Consent needed. Fallback: a staff member does the loop.

**Tone.** In-store energy. Shelves, boxes, hands, people. This is the pillar that makes the store feel alive.

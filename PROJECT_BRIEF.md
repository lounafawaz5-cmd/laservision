# Social Media Project Brief: Laser Vision & Zeiss

Running notes on how we work. Updated as decisions are made. This feeds the
monthly-planning skill we build at the end.

## Brands
| Brand | What it is | Trello |
|---|---|---|
| Laser Vision | Laser eye center (eye-related medical services) | Own board |
| Zeiss | Eyewear (sunglasses, eyeglasses) | Own board |

## Channels & cadence
- Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook
- About 3 posts per week per brand
- Languages: English and Arabic, chosen per post (user flags in advance what works)

## Workflow
1. **Pre-production** (Claude + user)
   - Analytics on both accounts: numbers, best hooks, captions, formats → patterns to replicate
   - Competitor/peer research → **every account, post, topic or format referenced gets its link saved** (see `references/`)
   - Content calendar and topics → Trello
   - Scripts, captions, cover-picture text
2. **Production**: user only (shoots with staff and equipment)
3. **Post-production** (Claude + user)
   - Reel editing with Remotion
   - Carousel design from the user's reference library

## How each step will be done (agreed)
- **Analytics:** through Claude in Chrome on the user's logged-in accounts (Instagram for both brands,
  Meta Business Suite, TikTok, Facebook). Scroll and scrape directly, then write a report.
- **Research:** through Chrome across TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Topics come from
  the consultant strategy doc's direction. Every reference is logged with its link.
- **Scripting:** after the calendar is set, discuss length and style. Transcribe past reels
  (user files or pulled from Instagram) to learn the voice. One script template per brand.
- **Captions and cover text:** one standard formula per brand, applied to every post.
- **Reels:** set up the most streamlined pipeline. Decide on reel template types together
  (shared templates vs. unique one-offs).
- **Carousels:** the user makes a references folder. Leading idea: text on the Trello card →
  auto-generated slides. Final method decided together.

Full setup checklist: `NEXT_SESSION_SETUP.md`.

## Working rules
- One step at a time; don't run ahead into later stages.
- A consultant strategy document exists (skeleton only); analytics and research fill it in.
- Medical claims for Laser Vision need care (no guarantees or "painless/perfect" promises).

## Raw footage
- Lives on the user's iPad files, Google Drive, and an SD card
- Session is cloud-based for now; the user will later connect Claude to their desktop, where the SD card and files can be reached

## Later (not now)
- Weekly planning with fixed days (e.g. a Laser Vision shoot day, a separate edit day). Do this last.
- Wider marketing beyond social media: newsletters, pop-ups, etc.
- Final deliverable: a monthly-planning skill that runs the whole cycle quickly.

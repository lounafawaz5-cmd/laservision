# Post caption (LaserVision, eye exam vs vision test reel)

Seeing clearly doesn't always mean your eyes are healthy.
A vision test checks how well you see and whether you need glasses or contact lenses. A full eye exam goes further: it looks at the cornea, the lens, the retina, the optic nerve and the pressure inside your eye.
Some conditions, like glaucoma and diabetic retinopathy, can start without symptoms. Ask your eye doctor which exam is right for you, then book it at LaserVision.

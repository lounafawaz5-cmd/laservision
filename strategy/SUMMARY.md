## Hard rules
**LaserVision never:**
- shows doctors on camera or names or compares doctors (no exceptions until further notice)
- promises results or outcomes
- uses fear-based language
- says "best/first in Lebanon" without a cleared claim
- uses retail or discount language (exception being explored: hearing tests, hearing aids, ear plugs)
- Always: "consult your eye doctor first, then book at LaserVision."

**ZVC never:**
- uses the **blue ZEISS block logo** in content the store produces. The name "ZEISS Vision Center" is fine; the logo is the **"ZEISS Vision Center by Laser Vision" wordmark** (`brand/zeiss/zvc-wordmark-*.png`).
- makes medical claims or talks about surgery, conditions or treatment
- lectures (one line of product knowledge, then back to frames)
- hard-sells or runs constant discounts (promotions allowed, but chosen sparingly)

**Cross-brand loop:** LaserVision points to ZVC for glasses; ZVC points to LaserVision for eye tests and anything medical.

## Priorities from the owners
- **Grow (LaserVision):** hearing tests and hearing aids, dry eye tests and treatment, the one-stop-shop model (doctor → test → glasses or surgery under one roof)
- Aesthetic laser hasn't grown in a while, so it doesn't need to stay in the background
- Refractive surgery is on a downtrend worldwide (lower confidence). Explore what social media people are exposed to.
- Cataract at HDF is near capacity, so don't push it
- 12-month targets: **4 hearing aids a month, 3 glasses a day**, more patients in Hazmieh

## Practical answers
- Language: depends on who's filming. **Lebanese Arabic most often** in reels. **Subtitles are in Arabic on both accounts** (decided 2026-10-22; overrides the ZVC board's "subtitle in English"). ZVC: a natural Lebanese-English mix. **Subtitles on everything.**
- **Carousels are always in English** (both brands).
- Filming: all branches including HDF (coordinate with staff); surgery and prep rooms yes, **sterilisation room no**. Patients need consent; surgery needs patient **and** doctor consent.
- Before and after: maybe for some surgeries and for hearing aids
- Subtle tech comparisons are OK (no names)
- Influencers and creators: yes, especially for ZVC
- Platforms: Instagram (main), TikTok (both exist), Facebook (older audience, Tripoli and Saida). LinkedIn is for the doctor track only.
- Approver: **Georges** (single approver). Crisis spokespeople: Gaby, Marc, Georges.
- Pinned posts: LaserVision = service map, Marc's story, locations and contact. ZVC = what we sell, meet the store, how to find us.
- Monthly report per account: followers, reach, engagement, top posts, WhatsApp enquiries

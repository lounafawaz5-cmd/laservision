Built from `strategy/SUMMARY.md` and modeled on the ZVC board. Nothing is in Trello yet.

## The rules this calendar follows
- **Cadence:** 3 posts a week, **Mon / Wed / Fri**. ZVC posts Tue / Thu / Sat, so the two accounts never post on the same day.
- **Mix:** Educational 14 (47%) · Credibility 10 (33%) · Storytelling 6 (20%). Target: 50 / 30 / 20.
- **Formats:** 22 reels + 8 carousels (about one carousel a week, in the reference carousel style).
- **Priorities from the owners:** hearing tests and aids (4 posts), dry eye (2), aesthetic laser (2, it hasn't grown in a while), and calm, trust-building refractive content (3). **Cataract is not pushed** because HDF is near capacity.
- **Never:** doctors on camera, promises about results, fear, "best/first in Lebanon", retail or discount language.
- **Every post:** WhatsApp **+961 81 54 20 20**, Arabic subtitles, Instagram + Facebook + TikTok.
- **On camera:** Marc, Georges, reception, a nurse or technician, the audiologist. Same faces week after week.

## Format guides (the LaserVision versions of the ZVC "Guidelines" cards)
| Format | How to film it | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **Your doctor said X, here's what happens next** | Staff to camera in scrubs, cut with B-roll of each step in order (reference reel style). | 30–45 s |
| **Myth vs fact** | Myth on screen as text, one person answers calmly in one breath, ends with "your doctor decides". | 20–30 s |
| **Your first visit / How to reach us** | Reception to camera, walking: door → desk → waiting area. Parking shot first. | 30 s |
| **Inside the room / What this machine does** | Hands, machines and screens. Text on screen, no voice or a short voiceover. | 20–30 s |
| **Meet the team** | One person, name and role on screen, one honest line. | 15–20 s |
| **Marc's chapters** | Marc seated, natural light, one chapter per month, old photos as B-roll if available. | 45–60 s |
| **Educational carousel** | Reference carousel style: navy, one idea per slide, last slide "Consult your eye doctor" + WhatsApp. | 5–7 slides |

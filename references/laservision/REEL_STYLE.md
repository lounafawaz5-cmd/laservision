# LaserVision Reel Style (from reference reel)

Reference: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdWtN4aOg2G/ (42 s, dry eye / IPL). Frames: `reel-DdWtN4aOg2G/frames.png`
Caption on post: "Your eye doctor decides which refractive surgery is right for your eyes. WhatsApp: 81 54 20 20"

## Picture
- **Talking head:** staff member in navy LaserVision scrubs (logo embroidered on chest), framed from the waist up, centered, looking at camera.
- **Set:** clinic interior with a soft blue backlight and a vertical-slat wall. Shallow depth of field.
- **B-roll cut-ins** every few seconds: the procedure (patient on the bed, IPL mask, gloved hands), the device, a test at the slit lamp, the diagnostic screen close-up.
- Occasional **illustration overlay** for a medical term (e.g. a meibomian gland graphic in a circle).
- No title card at the start, no branded end card. It opens straight on the speaker and ends on the speaker.

## Subtitles
- **Language:** the spoken language (Lebanese Arabic). English medical terms stay in English inside the Arabic line, in CAPS (IPL, DRYNESS, MEIBOMIAN GLANDS, TEST).
- **Chunking:** 1–3 words at a time, synced to speech.
- **Look:** white, bold, no background box, soft shadow. Quick fade in.
- **Position:** centered horizontally, about 60% of the way down (chest height), clear of the face.

## Pacing
- Speaker shots of 2–5 s alternating with B-roll of 1–3 s.
- Length 30–45 s.

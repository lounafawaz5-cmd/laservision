# LaserVision Carousel Style (from reference carousel)

Reference: https://www.instagram.com/p/DdpHNkOjl2S/ ("Seeing flashes of light?", 6 slides). Slides: `carousel-DdpHNkOjl2S/`

## Format
- **1200 × 1500 (4:5)**, English only, font **Outfit**.
- 5–7 slides. One idea per slide.

## Layout (every slide)
- **Background:** deep navy (#00303F), with a photo or illustration filling the lower half and fading up into the navy.
- **Top right:** slide counter `01/06` with a row of dots underneath (filled dot = current slide).
- **Headline:** top left, white, Outfit Bold, sentence case, 2–3 lines, ends with a period.
- **Body:** directly under the headline, white, Outfit Regular/Light, 2–4 lines.
- **Logo:** white LaserVision logo, small, bottom center.

## Cover (slide 1)
- Headline in ALL CAPS, phrased as a question ("SEEING FLASHES OF LIGHT?").
- One-line subhead ("Here's what your eyes are telling you").
- A full-bleed atmospheric photo (bokeh lights) under the text.

## Middle slides
- Medical illustrations get thin white callout lines and labels.
- Real clinic photos (staff at a machine) are preferred over stock where possible.

## Last slide
- The final fact, then **"Consult your eye doctor. They'll know where to send you."**
- Footer bar: logo bottom left · branches "Hazmieh · Saida · Tripoli · Hôtel-Dieu" · WhatsApp icon **+961 81 54 20 20** bottom right.
- Photo of the clinic reception.

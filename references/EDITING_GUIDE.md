# Video editing guide (both accounts)

Source: `references/Video_Editing_Guide.pdf` (Oct 8, 2026, CapCut settings). **This is the caption standard for every reel.**
Both accounts share font, captions, transitions and sound effects; only the glow color differs.

## Captions
- Font: **Montserrat Arabic Bold** (covers English and Arabic). Bundled now: Latin Montserrat Bold only;
  Arabic falls back to Noto Sans Arabic until the Montserrat Arabic file is added to `reels/public/fonts/`.
- Template: **word-by-word fade**.
- White text, **glow** (CapCut standard preset, intensity 30, range 58):
  - LaserVision **#005D83**
  - ZVC **#0C188D**

## Transitions (CapCut names)
Mix (default crossfade) · Echo Fade (Pro) · Film Reject (energetic/reveal) · Acrylic Fades (calm, clinical).
Without Pro: Mix instead of Echo Fade.

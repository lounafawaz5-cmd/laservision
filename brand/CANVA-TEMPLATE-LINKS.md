# Editable Canva templates for ZVC and LaserVision

Prepared 29 September 2026.

This index contains 41 editable Canva design templates prepared for your handover. The ZVC designs, LaserVision designs, and designs shared by both brands are listed below. A template link copies the entire design, including every page, into the Canva account you choose.

## How to use a template

1. Sign in to the Canva account where you want to keep your copy.
2. Open the template link and select **Use template**.
3. Rename the new design and place it in the appropriate folder in your Canva account.

Keep this index with your intended team. Anyone with a template link can use it to make a copy. The [Canva source folder](https://www.canva.com/folder/FAHWkoIWjys) is also provided for reference; opening it may require separate folder access.

These are editable source designs. The finished social media archive is organized separately in the `Published` and `Unpublished` folders. A Canva design may contain several posts or carousel pages, and this index also includes moodboards and working designs.

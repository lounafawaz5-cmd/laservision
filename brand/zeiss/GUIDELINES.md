# ZEISS Vision Center: Visual Identity

Colors from the official ZEISS Brand Portal:
https://brand.zeiss.com/en/brand-design-elements/colors/basic-colors

## Colors
| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | White | **#FFFFFF** | Dominant |
| Primary | Black | **#000000** | Dominant |
| Secondary | ZEISS Indigo | **#0F2DB3** | Logo color; used sparingly like the other secondaries |
| Secondary | ZEISS Azure | **#0072EF** | Interactive elements (links, buttons) |
| Secondary | ZEISS Violet | **#8F3CFB** | Accents on non-interactive elements |
| Accent | ZEISS Lime | **#D9E906** | Highlights only, far less often than the secondaries |

## ZEISS color rules
- Black and white dominate. Text is black on white or white on black. Colored text only to draw attention.
- Don't place secondary colors next to each other, don't overuse them, don't use tints, don't make colors transparent.
- Gradients: one secondary color fading to black or white only.
- No colored backgrounds behind images or video.

## Logo
- Use the **"ZEISS Vision Center by Laser Vision" wordmark**: `zvc-wordmark-dark.png` (on light) / `zvc-wordmark-white.png` (on dark).
- **Never the blue ZEISS block logo** in original store content.
- The store name "ZEISS Vision Center" may be written in text.

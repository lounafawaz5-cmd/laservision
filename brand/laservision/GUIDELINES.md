# LaserVision: Visual Identity (from Brand Guidelines, Dec 2025)

Source: `LaserVision_Brand_Guidelines.pdf`

## Colors
| Role | Name | HEX | Pantone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Blue | **#005D83** | 634 C |
| Primary | Deep navy | **#00303F** | 539 C |
| Primary | Light gray | **#F1F2F2** | 663 C |
| Secondary | Teal | **#30A4BF** | 311 C |
| Secondary | Mint | **#B6DFE5** | 635 C |
| Secondary | White | **#FFFFFF** | White |

Primary colors dominate (backgrounds, type, major elements). Secondary colors are accents and highlights only and must never overpower the primary palette.

## Logos
| Version | Use | File |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (horizontal) | Intro and end of videos, large formats | `logo.png`, `logo-transparent.png`, `logo-white.png` |
| Secondary (stacked) | Profile headers, narrow spaces, watermarks | not supplied yet |
| Submark (circular LV) | Profile photo, watermarks on photos and videos | not supplied yet |
| Logo mark (LV) | Social media posts, motion graphics | not supplied yet |

- Full color on clean, light or neutral backgrounds. White version on blue, navy or busy images.
- Keep clear space around the logo (one sunburst unit on each side).
- **Never:** recolor, distort, add effects or shadows, change transparency, blur, rotate, add stroke or outline, or remove any part.

## Typography (brand guidelines)
- **Raleway:** logo, headings, prominent messaging
- **General Sans** (Light/Regular/Medium/Bold): body, captions
- **Social carousels use Outfit** (Light/Medium/Bold) by Luna's decision; it applies to both accounts.

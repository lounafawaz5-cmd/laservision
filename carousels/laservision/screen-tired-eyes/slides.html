Screen-tired
eyes?

5 habits that help.

02/06
1Tip one

The 20-20-20 rule.

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds.

03/06
2Tip two

Blink more.

We blink far less when we stare at a screen, and fewer blinks let the eye dry out. Every so often, close your eyes fully and slowly.

04/06
3Tip three

Fix your setup.

Keep the screen about an arm's length away, with the top of the screen at or just below eye level.

05/06
4Tip four

Mind the light.

Match your screen's brightness to the room, and set your desk so light from windows doesn't glare off the screen.

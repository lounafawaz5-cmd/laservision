BeforeStart with the right tests

Preparation starts
before procedure day.

  • Complete the requested eye tests
  • Consult your treating ophthalmologist
  • Share your eye history, medical conditions and medications
  • Ask every question you have

Your ophthalmologist determines whether laser correction is suitable for you and which procedure is appropriate.

BeforeBefore your measurements

Contact lenses?
Follow your
instructions.

Contact lenses can temporarily affect the shape of your cornea and the accuracy of your measurements.

  • Switch to glasses when instructed
  • Follow the exact timeline provided by your eye-care team
  • Do not choose the stopping period yourself

Timing varies according to the type of contact lens and procedure.

BeforeProcedure day

Keep the day
simple.

  • Arrive with clean eyes and face
  • Avoid eye makeup, creams and perfume if instructed
  • Follow any medication instructions provided
  • Arrange for someone to take you home
  • Wear comfortable clothing
Do not plan to drive yourself home.
AfterThe first hours

Rest. Protect.
Don’t rub.

  • Go home and rest
  • Use the prescribed drops exactly as directed
  • Do not touch or rub your eyes
  • Wear the protective shield if provided
  • Expect temporary blur or irritation

Contact your treating ophthalmologist if symptoms become severe or worsen.

AfterDuring recovery

Follow your
recovery plan.

  • Attend every follow-up appointment
  • Continue your prescribed drops
  • Wear sunglasses outdoors
  • Wait for clearance before driving
  • Keep water, dust and products away from your eyes
Recovery differs between LASIK, PRK, Femto and other procedures.
